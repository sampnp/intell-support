Acme Cloud - Security and Data Privacy

This document describes how Acme Cloud protects your files, how long we keep data, how to delete your account and how to export a copy of everything you store with us.

1. Encryption

All files are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer whenever they move between your devices and our servers. At rest, files are stored encrypted with AES-256. Encryption keys are managed in a dedicated key management service and are rotated regularly.

Enterprise workspaces can bring their own key (BYOK). With BYOK, the workspace's master key lives in the customer's own key management system; revoking that key makes the workspace's files unreadable to everyone, including Acme Cloud staff.

Acme Cloud does not offer end-to-end encryption for regular folders, because features such as full-text search, previews and the web editor need to read file contents on our servers. For highly sensitive material, Enterprise customers can create "Vault" folders, which are end-to-end encrypted and are not searchable.

2. Who can access your files

Acme Cloud employees cannot open your files during normal operations. Access to customer data is limited to a small security team, requires a documented reason and is recorded in an audit log. Support agents can see file names and sizes only when you explicitly grant temporary access while troubleshooting a ticket.

Workspace administrators on Business and Enterprise plans can see the activity log for their workspace, including sign-ins, shares and file deletions.

3. Data retention

Deleted files stay in the trash for 30 days on Free and Personal plans and for 120 days on Business and Enterprise plans. You can restore them from the trash at any time during that period. After the retention period, files are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by support.

Previous versions of a file are kept for the same period as the trash. Restoring an old version creates a new version and does not remove later ones.

Backups of our storage systems are kept for 35 days for disaster recovery only. We do not restore individual files from these backups on request.

4. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings > Account > Delete account. Before deleting, cancel any paid subscription; deleting the account does not issue a refund for the remaining billing period.

After you confirm, the account is deactivated immediately and you can no longer sign in. Your files and personal data are permanently erased within 30 days. During those 30 days you can contact support to cancel the deletion.

If you are a member of a Business workspace, only the workspace administrator can delete your account. Files you created in the workspace belong to the workspace and are kept according to its retention policy.

5. Exporting your data

You can download a copy of all your data at any time. Go to Settings > Account > Export data and choose what to include: files, file version history and account information. We prepare the export in the background and email you a download link when it is ready, usually within 24 hours for accounts under 100 GB.

The export is a set of ZIP archives of at most 10 GB each. The download link stays valid for 7 days. Exports of very large accounts are split into several archives that can be downloaded separately.

6. Compliance and reporting a security issue

Acme Cloud is audited annually against SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001. Data processing agreements for GDPR are available to Business and Enterprise customers in the admin console.

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, email security@acmecloud.example. Please do not post details publicly until we have confirmed a fix.
